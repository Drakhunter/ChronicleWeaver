# ChronicleWeaver — Customer Vision & Feature Wishlist
**Persona:** Hardcore WoW Lore Enthusiast, Explorer & Environmental Storytelling Fan  
**Target Game:** World of Warcraft: Forever (`Interface: 16001`)

## 1. Why I Want This Addon (The Problem)
When I play WoW: Forever, I want to immerse myself in the world—especially with all the brand-new Classic+ dungeons, zones, and mysteries. Right now, existing lore addons fall into two frustrating traps:
- **The "Wiki Dump" Trap:** Addons stuffed with AI-generated essays that spoil bosses and zones before I even set foot in them.
- **The "Broken Book Saver" Trap:** Simple book-collector scripts that overwrite Page 1 when I turn to Page 2, dump everything into an ugly unsearchable list, and never tell me *where* I found a plaque three weeks later.

I want an addon that feels like **an Archaeologist's Field Journal and Detective Board**—something that rewards me for exploring, saves every real in-game text I find, and helps me connect the dots on my own!

---

## 2. My Core Wishes & Needs

### A. Spoiler-Free Discovery ("Earn My Lore")
- **Don't spoil things I haven't seen:** If an NPC, dungeon, or relic is in my journal, keep the story hidden until I actually walk into that zone, target that boss, or loot that item.
- **Subtle World Feedback:** When I hover over an important NPC, show a small note in their tooltip so I know they matter to the story. When I discover something new, give me a cool pop-up banner I can click to read right away.
- **Let me break the rules if I want:** Give me a simple toggle button to show/hide spoilers whenever I'm in the mood to just browse everything.

### B. The Ultimate Book, Scroll & Plaque Collector
- **Save every page automatically:** Whenever I click an in-game book, monument, gravestone, or scroll, save all its pages automatically as I flip through them—never lose Page 1 when I read Page 2!
- **Remember where I found it:** Stamp every book or plaque with the exact Zone, Subzone, and Map Coordinates where I stood when I read it.
- **Take me back there:** Give me a "Pin to Map" button so if I want to revisit a shrine or gravestone later, it drops a waypoint pin right on my World Map.

### C. Connective Tissue ("The Detective Board")
- **Highlight names & places I know:** When I'm reading an in-game book, automatically highlight known bosses, dungeons, or zones in gold text so I immediately spot the connection.
- **1-Click Jump Links:** At the bottom of any entry, show me clickable tags for related bosses, zones, or books so I can jump straight between them without typing in the search bar.
- **"Dark Souls-Style" Clue Synthesis:** If I read an ancient plaque in Ironforge AND target a specific boss in the Hall of Thanes, combine those two clues automatically to unlock a special "Deduction" entry that connects the mystery!

### D. My Personal Field Journal
- **Per-Entry Notes:** I want a personal notepad at the bottom of every entry where I can type my own theories, roleplay notes, or reminders—and have it auto-save instantly.
- **Clean, Fast Navigation:** Give me simple category tabs (`All`, `Zone`, `Dungeon`, `NPC`, `Relic`, `Book`, `Deduction`), a live search bar, and smooth scrolling so my journal still feels snappy when I have 200+ books saved.
